Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 10, 2026 • Application: com.sholotech.qrbarcodescanner.free

Core Privacy Guarantee: Zero Data Collection. What you scan, create, and save stays exclusively on your device.

1. Introduction & Commitment

At SholoTech, your privacy is our primary priority. We built QR Barcode Scanner & Generator with a strict on-device architecture: we collect zero personal data, require no accounts, and never transmit your scanned or generated codes to remote servers.

2. Permissions Requested & Their Purpose

Our app requests only the minimal Android operating system permissions necessary to deliver barcode functionality:

  • Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA): Required solely to stream live frames to the on-device ML Kit barcode scanner. Frames are processed in volatile memory in real time and immediately discarded. No photos, videos, or camera recordings are ever saved, stored, or transmitted.
  • Read Photos / Media (Gallery Selection): Accessed only when you explicitly choose "Import from gallery". The image is read into local memory, analyzed for codes, and is never uploaded.
  • Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used solely to provide haptic feedback when a barcode is recognized.
  • Wi-Fi State (CHANGE_WIFI_STATE / ACCESS_WIFI_STATE): When you scan a Wi-Fi QR code and tap "Connect to Wi-Fi", the app uses standard Android APIs to configure the network locally on your device.
  • Internet Access (INTERNET): Only utilized when you choose to open an external web link in your default browser, or to inspect URL safety headers. The app does not run background analytics.

3. Local Data Storage & User Control

All data generated while using the application is stored entirely on your local device:

  • Sandboxed SQLite Database: Your scan history is stored in private, sandboxed local storage on your phone. No cloud sync exists.
  • Complete User Control: You can delete any individual scan record by swiping, or delete all history with one tap in Settings → Clear History.
  • Complete Purge: Uninstalling the application permanently deletes 100% of the app's local data from your device.

4. Payments & UPI QR Codes

When you scan a UPI payment QR code (such as Google Pay, PhonePe, or Paytm):

  • The app parses the standard upi://pay link to show the merchant name and provide a 1-tap payment button.
  • Tapping "Pay via UPI" opens Android's native app chooser to launch your official payment app.
  • We do not collect, process, or store bank accounts, credit/debit cards, UPI PINs, or transaction histories. All financial transactions take place securely inside your authorized banking application.

5. Advertisements & Google AdMob (Free Version)

The Free edition of QR Barcode Scanner & Generator uses Google AdMob to display banner advertisements, enabling us to offer core scanning and generator features for free:

  • Google AdMob SDK: AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), general IP location, and app performance data to serve ads and prevent fraud in compliance with Google's policies.
  • Google Ad Technologies: To learn how Google uses data when you use our app, review the Google Privacy & Terms for Advertising.
  • Pro Upgrade (Ad-Free): Users who upgrade to the Pro edition enjoy a completely 100% ad-free experience with zero advertising network requests.
  • We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal scan data or generated codes.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our app is a general-purpose utility suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect, store, or solicit personal information from children under 13. Because the application operates with a zero-data-collection model, it fully complies with COPPA and international child protection standards.
